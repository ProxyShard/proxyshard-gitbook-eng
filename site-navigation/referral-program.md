# Referral program

The `Affiliate` section contains your personal referral link. Click `Copy link` and share it with the user you want to invite.

You receive a 10% level 1 commission on purchases made by users you invite.

Level 2 commission is available only to influencers, large teams, and other partners with their own media resources. Once enabled, you receive a percentage of purchases made by customers referred by your level 1 referrals. The commission rate is set individually. If you have any questions about level 2, contact us through `Live Chat`. We will be happy to help.

To customize the end of the referral link, click the pencil icon next to it and save the new value. The same page shows your earnings, unpaid commissions, and the number of referrals at each level.

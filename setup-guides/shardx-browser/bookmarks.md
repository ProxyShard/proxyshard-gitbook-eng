# Bookmarks

<mark style="color:purple;">**Bookmarks**</mark> lets you set up bookmarks once and hand them out to every profile or to the profiles in a chosen folder. Click <mark style="color:purple;">**+ Add a bookmark**</mark> (or <mark style="color:purple;">**+ New bookmark**</mark> at the top). <mark style="color:purple;">**Import from Chrome**</mark> brings over the bookmarks from your Chrome.

<figure>
  <picture>
    <source srcset="../../.gitbook/assets/shardbrowser-bookmarks-empty_black.png" media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)">
    <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/shardbrowser-bookmarks-empty_white.png" alt="Bookmarks section">
  </picture>
  <figcaption>Bookmarks section</figcaption>
</figure>

In the <mark style="color:purple;">**New bookmark**</mark> window, enter the <mark style="color:purple;">**URL**</mark>, the <mark style="color:purple;">**Title**</mark> and the <mark style="color:purple;">**Folder**</mark>. <mark style="color:purple;">**Every profile**</mark> adds the bookmark to all profiles. Click <mark style="color:purple;">**Save**</mark>.

<figure>
  <picture>
    <source srcset="../../.gitbook/assets/shardbrowser-bookmark-new_black.png" media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)">
    <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/shardbrowser-bookmark-new_white.png" alt="Fill in the fields and click Save">
  </picture>
  <figcaption>Fill in the fields and click Save</figcaption>
</figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
The bookmark shows up in the bookmarks bar of every profile in the chosen folder, inside a bookmark folder called **ShardBrowser**, the next time the profile starts. Bookmarks you added in a profile yourself stay as they are.
{% endhint %}
